# Contribution Guidelines

Thank you for suggesting a site. Please check that:

- It works in an ordinary browser, needs no download, and can be tried without an account or payment. Mark anything partly paid.
- It is fun, beautiful, surprising or interesting by itself, not a tool for getting work done.
- It is not mainly advertising, gambling, a store, or a site that asks for personal details.
- You add one site per pull request, at the end of the section it fits, in the form `- [Name](https://address) - What you do there.`
- The description is your own, short, starts with a capital letter and ends with a full stop. Say what a visitor does or sees, without praise words.
- You say so if you made the site.

Sites that stop working are removed. If you notice one, an issue or a pull request is welcome.

By contributing you agree that your contribution is released under [CC0](LICENSE).
